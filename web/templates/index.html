Advertising Preferences

Understand how advertisements are displayed across PhishGuard and how your subscription affects your experience.

◎
YOUR PHISHGUARD EXPERIENCE

Advertising is determined by your plan

PhishGuard first checks your subscription eligibility and the consent signal supplied by its configured advertising consent platform. Advertising scripts are not requested when that platform is missing, unavailable, or reports that consent has not been granted.

1. Ads on the Free plan

Users on the Free plan may see advertisements in supported areas of the dashboard. Advertising helps support the operation and continued development of PhishGuard.

2. Ad-free paid plans

Eligible paid subscriptions, such as Pro and Business, are designed to provide an ad-free experience while the subscription is active. Your plan and billing status determine eligibility.

You can review your current subscription, plan status and available upgrades in .

3. Advertising relevance and personalization

Where supported by the advertising provider and applicable law, ads may be contextual or personalized. Personalization may depend on provider settings, browser controls and consent requirements in your region.

PhishGuard does not use your scanned URLs as an advertising preference profile. URL scanning and threat detection remain separate from advertising.

4. Cookies and advertising technologies

Before AdSense can load, PhishGuard requires its configured consent-management platform (CMP) to provide valid IAB Transparency and Consent Framework (TCF) signals. The site applies a strict global gate and checks for affirmative consent to purposes 1, 3 and 4 and Google as an advertising vendor for every visitor. If the CMP is missing, unavailable, or consent is declined, the AdSense script is not requested. Third-party advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies only in accordance with the consent signals, their own policies and applicable law. For personalized ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google requires a Google-certified CMP integrated with the IAB TCF. Advertising Preferences is an informational page, not a CMP or a substitute for the consent dialog.

5. Changing your advertising experience

Use the consent controls displayed by the configured CMP to review or change your advertising consent. You can also change your subscription in Plans & Billings; eligible Pro and Business subscriptions are ad-free while active. Rejecting advertising consent does not restrict URL scanning or account access.

6. Third-party providers

When advertisements are served, the advertising provider may process limited technical information such as browser or device data in accordance with its own privacy terms. Review the provider's privacy information for details about its processing practices.

Important note

Advertising availability can change based on your plan, subscription status, regional requirements and the configuration of advertising services. PhishGuard's security scanning features are not conditional on interacting with an advertisement.

AI-POWERED CYBERSECURITY PLATFORM

PhishGuard.

PhishGuard helps users assess suspicious website links before opening them. It combines a locally available machine-learning model, defensive URL rules and optional online threat-intelligence checks in an offline-first security workflow.

VERSION 1.0.0

What PhishGuard does

Core capabilities available in the security workspace

⌕

URL security scanning

Submit a website URL for analysis and receive a clear Legitimate or Phishing assessment, together with a risk score and model confidence where available.

✳

Machine-learning detection

A trained classification model examines URL patterns and extracted characteristics that can help distinguish suspicious links from ordinary website addresses.

⌘

Layered security checks

Local detection rules add defensive checks such as known blocked indicators, trusted allowlist handling and suspicious address patterns alongside the model assessment.

◉

Offline-first protection

The local model and available rules can perform scans without requiring a live threat-intelligence connection. This helps keep the core detection workflow available when internet access is limited.

⇄

Optional online intelligence

When Online Mode and provider configuration are available, PhishGuard can use external threat-intelligence lookups to add current context to a URL assessment.

Privacy and security approach

PhishGuard is designed around an offline-first workflow. Local scans use the detection resources available on the device or server. External lookups are optional and depend on Online Mode and the configuration of supported providers. Avoid submitting private or confidential URLs unless you understand how any enabled external provider may process lookup requests.

Important security disclaimer

PhishGuard is a defensive decision-support tool, not a guarantee of website safety. Detection depends on the model, local rules and intelligence data available at the time of a scan. A Legitimate result means that the current checks did not identify a threat; a new, compromised or previously unseen phishing page may still be missed. Always verify the website address and use safe browsing practices.
